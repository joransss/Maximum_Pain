## Working principles
1. Document the full intended game now; implement it in small, dependency-ordered milestones.
2. Treat Roblox Studio as the source of truth for the live place, this vault as the source of design intent, and `CLAUDE.md` as the working rules for Claude Code.
3. Server-authoritative competitive gameplay: damage, ammo, fire rate, cooldowns, scoring, rewards and match state are validated on the server.
4. Prototype with greybox assets before investing in polished art, animation or effects.
5. Do not publish the experience or make destructive edits without explicit approval.
6. Do not mark a feature complete until its acceptance tests have passed.

## Visual direction
Gritty urban crime thriller; grounded materials; strong silhouette readability; cinematic framing used sparingly; restrained palette of charcoal, concrete grey, dirty cream, amber/orange and muted red. Gameplay clarity takes priority over visual effects.
