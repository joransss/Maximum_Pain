# Core Gameplay Loop

## Match Loop
1. Join lobby.
2. Choose a loadout.
3. Spawn into the map.
4. Move between cover and firing lanes.
5. Track opponents and manage ammunition.
6. Use a dive or bullet-time ability to win a key engagement.
7. Earn eliminations and support objectives.
8. Finish the round.
9. Show results and restart.

## Moment-to-Moment Loop
**Observe → Position → Aim → Fire → Reposition → Recover**

## Player Decisions
- Engage now or reposition?
- Use bullet time now or save it?
- Dive across a dangerous lane or keep cover?
- Reload now or after moving to safety?

## Feedback Needed
- Hit marker or hit reaction
- Damage feedback
- Ammo count
- Ability charge / cooldown
- Kill feed
- Clear round-end message

## Questions to Test
- Is it too easy to rush without using cover?
- Does bullet time create a meaningful decision?
- Can players understand why they lost a fight?
