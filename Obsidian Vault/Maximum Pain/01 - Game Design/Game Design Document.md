# Game Design Document

## High Concept
A competitive Roblox third-person shooter focused on responsive close-to-mid-range gunfights, combat dives, line-of-sight Bullet Time, tactical cover and short objective-driven matches.

## Design Pillars
1. **Responsive combat:** shooting and movement should feel immediate.
2. **Readable action:** players should understand who hit them and why.
3. **Mobility with risk:** dives create opportunities but leave players vulnerable during recovery.
4. **Local slow motion:** Bullet Time affects connected sightline fights, not the entire server clock.
5. **Counterplay:** cover, positioning and timing can counter strong abilities.
6. **Fair multiplayer:** server authority for damage, ammo, cooldowns, currency and scoring.
7. **Cosmetic-only monetisation:** paid content must not create competitive stat advantages.

## First Playable Scope
- One mode: Free-for-All or Team Deathmatch
- One compact original map with solid cover and flank routes
- One automatic weapon and one slower high-damage weapon
- Third-person aim, sprint, jump and Shoot-dodge
- Line-of-sight Bullet Time prototype
- Health, respawn, timer and scoreboard

## Later Feature Candidates
- Prone shooting after a dive
- Last Stand recovery mechanic
- Dual-wield sidearms
- Breakable glass and limited destructible cover
- Short, skippable final-kill camera
- Objective Chain and High-Value Target modes
- Tactical ability loadouts
- Skill scoring and daily/weekly challenges
- Earnable cosmetic currency and cases, with policy-compliant odds disclosure

## Out of Scope Until Core Combat Works
- Large open world
- Ranked matchmaking
- Player trading
- Fully destructible buildings
- Full replay recording
- Large weapon/ability roster
- Complex progression before combat is stable

## Originality and IP
Use broad genre inspiration—cinematic third-person gunplay and slow-motion combat—but create original characters, names, maps, UI, audio, animations and visual identity. Do not use Max Payne/Rockstar logos, game assets, soundtrack or copied character likenesses.

## Open Questions
- Free-for-All or Team Deathmatch for the first test?
- What is the match length and score limit?
- Does Bullet Time chain through visible opponents? What are its radius and hop limits?
- Can players aim and fire during a dive?
- Is Last Stand enabled in competitive modes?
