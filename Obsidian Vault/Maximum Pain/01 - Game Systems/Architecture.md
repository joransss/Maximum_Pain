# Architecture

How the game's code is split and how the parts talk. Rebuilt 2026-10-31 for loadouts, stances, cover, shoot-dodge and Last Stand.
Related: [[Movement State Machine]] · [[Loadout and Dual Wielding]] · [[Cover System]] · [[Shoot-Dodge]] · [[Last Stand and Bullet Time]] · [[Animation Hooks]] · [[Test Arena]] · [[Changelog]]

## Ground rules
- **The server decides.** Stances, ammo, hits, damage, what is in each hand and Last Stand all happen on the server. Clients send requests and show the results.
- **Clients predict.** The client changes its own stance the moment a key is pressed, so the controls feel instant. The server checks each request and confirms it (silently), rejects it, or forces a different stance. See [[Movement State Machine]].
- **One stance at a time.** Every script asks `StanceRules` what the current stance allows, so no two scripts fight over the character, and each stance maps to one animation slot.
- **Timers run the game; animations follow.** Reloads, draws, dives and get-ups run on server timers, and the animations are stretched to fit them. See [[Animation Hooks]].
- **Rules live in shared modules.** The same code runs on both sides, so the client's prediction and the server's check agree.

## Where the code lives
### Shared (ReplicatedStorage.Modules)
| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `StanceRules` | The state machine: every stance, what it allows, its speed, crouch height, spread and recoil, and which stances it can change into. |
| `CoverRules` | Cover geometry: finding walls, high or low, edges, inside and outside corners, headroom. |
| `DodgeRules` | Shoot-dodge numbers and direction helpers. |
| `ShotRules` | Where a shot leaves from (the "virtual muzzle"), blind-fire paths over and round cover, and the stance and movement accuracy modifiers. |
| `LoadoutRules` | Slots, hold modes, the dual-wield drop penalty, pickups, the arm-injury rules, and when a switch passes the two-handed gun from hand to hand (`HANDOVER`, `handoverTimes`), as pure functions that check themselves. Since 2026-11-20 also the saved loadouts (`SAVED` = 5, opening at `SAVED_LEVELS` 1, 1, 1, 10, 25) and the four `PRESETS`; which guns a player may carry and buy (`canUse`, `canBuy`: the level reached, bought if it has a price); a loadout a client sends, checked whole (`checkGuns`, replaced by `checkLoadout` on 2026-11-21), and again at every spawn (`resolve`: a gun no longer allowed is swapped for the default's); the selection's guns (`selectedGuns`, now `selectedLoadout`); a loadout's weight and class (`weightOf`, `classOf`: Light up to `LIGHT_MAX` 10, Heavy from `HEAVY_MIN` 18; `MAX_WEIGHT` for the bar); the gun a spawn draws (`firstHold`); names tidied (`cleanName`, 20 characters, no control, hidden or noncharacters); `ID_LENGTH` (40), the longest a gun's or preset's id may be (checked against every one); `ArmoryState`, what the server sends the loadout screen. The screen and the server use the same functions, so the screen can't offer what the server refuses. Its self-checks try the rules with guns picked from `WeaponConfig`, so prices and levels can be tuned; the presets' weights as the plan worked them out only warn. Since 2026-11-21 a loadout (`Loadout`, `FIELDS`) also holds a Head, Chest and Gear item and a skill: `unlockOf`, `canUse` and `canBuy` cover guns, items and skills (their ids never clash, checked); `checkLoadout` checks a whole loadout a client sends (each field holding its own kind, at least one gun); `resolveKit` what a spawn really gets (`Kit`: an item not allowed left off, a skill swapped for Bullet Time), and `loadoutOfKit` a kit as a loadout again (the screen sends a change on top of it, so a field the player has lost can't block the rest); `selectedLoadout`, `presetLoadout`, `kitOf`, `sameKit`, `itemsOf`; `weightOf(guns, items)`; the presets carry items and skills; `unlocksBetween(from, to)`, what a level-up opens. |
| `VitalsRules` | Stamina and health regeneration and what each weight class does to them and to aiming (2026-11-20): `EFFECTS` per class (regeneration delay and rate, sprint time, aim-in time), `stepStamina` (a 6 s sprint at x1, filled in 4 s from 1 s after), `canSprint` (back to a quarter after running dry), `regenStep` (4% of a limb a second at x1, only above the red: `REGEN_FLOOR`), `classOf(character)` (the `WeightClass` attribute, Medium without one). Shared by `StanceService` and `LimbService` (server) and `CharacterController` and `CameraController` (client). Checks itself. Since 2026-11-21 `regenStep` takes items' modifiers (Stitches' delay, Bandages' rate). |
| `LastStandRules` | Last Stand numbers and who qualifies (since 2026-11-21: a full adrenaline segment, or none with the Pacemaker item). |
| `BulletTimeRules` | Who counts as seen, the time scales (0.3 for those seen, 0.6 for the activator), the aim penalty. Until 2026-11-21 also the meter's levels and how long each lasted; since then the meter is `AdrenalineRules`' and the durations `SkillConfig`'s. |
| `AdrenalineRules` | The adrenaline meter (2026-11-21): three segments of a third each (`segments`, `share`), what a death keeps (`kept`: the full ones), the gains (0.004 a point of damage, 0.25 a kill), a character's meter (`of`). Checks itself. |
| `SkillConfig` | Every skill (2026-11-21; the menu plan's section 6): its kind, name, description, price, its three levels (the player level each opens at, and its numbers: duration, heal, painkillers, damage dealt or taken), how many levels the game can do (`built`), its narration line. `usable` (the highest level a player may fire: bought or free, open at their level, built), `fireLevel` (that and the full segments), `describe` (a level's words, worked out from its numbers), `unlockOf`. Checks itself, with made-up skills for the rules so the real ones can be retuned. |
| `ItemConfig` | Every item (2026-11-21; section 5): its slot (Head, Chest, Gear), name, description, weight, level, price and what it does (`Effects`: armour by limb and side, spare magazines, Last Stand ammo, regeneration's delay and rate, painkillers, reload and draw time, Bullet Time proof, a Last Stand without adrenaline). `effectsOf` (a loadout's items together), `weightOf`, `carried(character)` (from its `ItemHead`, `ItemChest` and `ItemGear` attributes), `describe`, `unlockOf`. Checks itself; the plan's weights only warn. |
| `ShopConfig` | The shop's catalogue and the rules both sides share (2026-11-22; the menu plan's section 10): the five rarities (colour, 1 to 5 pips, the price a skin of it sells for directly, its weight in a crate), the 22 finishes' names (each a `SurfaceAppearance` in `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.Skins`), the 54 skins (one gun in a finish, or two finishes: the frame's and the moving parts' and magazine's) and the three crates of 18 (`CRATES`, in their shown order). `odds(crate, owned)` (the skins the player lacks, each its weight over the weight left), `roll` (a whole-number ticket over the same weights), `nextInOrder` (where random items are restricted), `progress`, `isComplete`; `stock(now)` (this week's featured skin, today's two, from the date with a fixed hash, so every server agrees) and `onSale` (with 30 s of grace after the change); `dress(model, gunId, skinId)` (the finishes onto a gun's parts: the server's guns and the screens' previews); `wearing` (the equipped skins a player owns and that fit); `fullName`, `price`, `skinsFor`. Checks itself: the catalogue's ids and references, the odds and the roll over every crate, and in Studio a year of storefronts (since 2026-11-23: it held up every join for most of a second); the plan's counts only warn. `stock` is kept per day and each id's hash worked out once, so the server can ask on every change. |
| `VaultRules` | What can be vaulted, the path over it (its apex rises with the avatar's legs), and the timing. The server only uses `find`, reaching `SERVER_SLACK` further for its lagging copy of a running character. |
| `RagdollRules` | The Euphoria-style death: muscles that pull toward a collapse and fade (see [[Euphoria-style Reactions and Ragdolls]]). |
| `AnimationConfig` | Every animation slot and the clip it plays (123 slots, 113 clips made in Blender), and each clip's published id (`IDS`, all 113 since 2026-11-14). In Studio a slot with no id, or a clip listed in `STALE`, plays its KeyframeSequence directly. Checks that every gun has a reload slot, and a one-handed gun a one-handed one. |
| `WeaponConfig`, `WeaponMath`, `LimbRules` | Gun numbers, spread and recoil maths, limb health rules (from the first milestone). `LimbRules.totalHealth`: every limb's health added up, which only drops on a hit (the menu's leave rule and UNDER FIRE use it; regeneration only raises it). Since 2026-11-20 `WeaponConfig` also holds each gun's description, unlock level and price (`Descriptions`, `Unlocks`) and an optional `WeightOverride`, and `WeaponMath` works out the loadout screen's five ratings (`ratings`), damage per second (`dps`) and each gun's weight from its stats (`power`, `weight`); both check themselves, and the weights the plan worked out (the M9 2, the AK-47 9, the M107 11) only warn, so tuning a gun can't stop the game. |
| `MatchRules` | The match's states and timings (placeholders: 8-minute free-for-all, 15 s between matches, 2 players in the fight to start), and `results`: who won and lost. A player counts only if they were in the match for half of it and fought (took `MIN_DAMAGE`, 10, off other players, or killed one); a match needs two who count (`MIN_CONTESTANTS`) to be won or lost at all; the top score wins, a tie at the top sharing it, unless everyone who counts tied (a draw: nobody wins, all lose). `Reason` says why a match didn't count for someone: Late, Idle or Alone (2026-11-19). Checks itself. |
| `ProgressionRules` | Levels, XP, Cash and titles: the level cap (50) and highest prestige (5); the level curve (`300 + 50 × (L − 1)`, 73,500 XP from 1 to 50); `REWARDS`, the same in XP and Cash (kill 10, win 1,000, loss 250, assist 5, headshot and shoot-dodge or bullet time +3, Last Stand +5, revenge +3); assists (a quarter of the damage); `PAID_PER_VICTIM` (5 kills and assists paid per victim a match, 2026-11-19); the Cash multiplier as a whole percentage (`cashPercent`: +5 every 5 levels, +20 per prestige), paid in whole Cash with the hundredths carried (`pay`); the titles (11 by level, 5 by prestige, ids that stay when names change). Also the types of what the server sends (`RewardNotice`, `MatchSummary`, `FeedEntry`). Checks itself. |
| `MenuRules` | The main menu's rules (2026-11-18): what a client may ask (`Play`, `Leave`, and since 2026-11-20 `Hold` and `Release`, the death screen's held respawn, at most `HOLD_LIMIT` 15 s after the death) and why the server may refuse; `LEAVE_SAFE_TIME` (5 s), `REDEPLOY_DELAY` (10 s), the gap between requests; the menu camera's shot clock (`shot`, `blackness`) and where its shots are (`shots`: `Workspace.MenuCams.<Map>`). Checks itself. |
| `UiAssets` | Every image the UI draws, by name: its size and its uploaded id (2026-11-18). No other script holds an asset id. Made by `Assets/Blender/UI/ui_lib.py` ([[Blender and Asset Pipeline#UI art for the menus]]). Since 2026-11-23 also the shop's six crate pictures (`UI_Crate_<line>` and `_Open`, named by `ShopConfig`'s crates). |

### Server (ServerScriptService)
| Script | Job |
|---|---|
| `CharacterServer` | Sets up each character in a fixed order (stance, limbs, loadout), takes stance and painkiller requests, runs the stance checks every frame. Replaces `LimbServer`. Since 2026-11-20 a character's guns come from `ArmoryService.spawnLoadout`; since 2026-11-21 its whole kit, set up as stance, items (`StatusEffects.setup`: the painkillers, spare ammo and weight depend on them), limbs, adrenaline (`AdrenalineService.setup`), guns, skill (`SkillService.setup`), ragdoll. Since 2026-11-22 the guns wear the skins the player equipped (`ShopService.wearing`). |
| `StatusEffects` | What items and skills do to a character (2026-11-21): sources of effects, a loadout's items for the whole life and a running skill for its time, which the other systems ask about (`multiplier`, `has`, `most`, `total`); `damageScale(attacker, victim, limb, part, direction)` for every hit (the attacker's boost, the victim's cuts, its armour on the head or the torso from in front or behind, which armour-piercing rounds ignore). Mirrors `NoSprint` and the items' ids (`ItemHead`, `ItemChest`, `ItemGear`) onto the character for clients. Sources go as characters go and timed ones as they run out. The queries walk the sources in place (no lists: damage asks several times a pellet), and `setup` works on a character not yet in the workspace. |
| `AdrenalineService` | The meter on the server (2026-11-21): `add`, `spend`, `drain`; a new life starts with the full segments the last death kept (`setup`); a Last Stand forfeits them from its start (`forfeit`), so it takes the whole meter however it ends. Since 2026-11-22 it keeps what a running skill has yet to spend (`claim`, from `SkillService`) and gives the meter less it (`free`), the part a Last Stand counts. |
| `SkillService` | Skills on the server (2026-11-21). Each character carries its loadout's skill (`setup`, the `Skill` attribute). The skill key (`SkillRequest`, no values, one each 0.25 s) fires it if its user is alive and able (not in a Last Stand or its wakeup), nothing of theirs runs, the profile still owns it and its level is open (read every time), at the most the full segments and the level allow; it spends that many segments, over its time or at once. What each kind does: Bullet Time through `BulletTimeService`; Hot Lead, Heavy Hand, Bulletproof and Open Tab a `StatusEffects` source; Second Wind `LimbService.heal` and `givePainkillers`; Gut Feeling the user's client marks everyone else. Tells the owner (`SkillEvent` "Used" or "Refused") and everyone's kill feed; keeps `ActiveSkill`, `ActiveSkillLevel`, `ActiveSkillEnds` while one runs. A death ends a running skill; a Last Stand settles it (the stand takes the meter, its unspent part with it), so it runs on without charging again. What a running skill has yet to spend is claimed (`AdrenalineService.claim`, kept up to date as it spends), so it opens no Last Stand. Bullet Time ends with its skill (`BulletTimeService.deactivate`). Answers the Studio hook `DebugBulletTime`. |
| `StanceService` | The server's state machine: checks requests, runs timed steps, re-checks cover, watches movement speed. The speed check (`checkSpeed`, reworked 2026-11-20 and 21 after the reviews) holds each stance to its own speed as the client sets it: the legs' walk speed (`MoveState` and `LimbRules.WALK_SPEED`: limping and crawling are slower) times the stance's share, 70% while aiming, a sprint's only while sprinting (and a second after the server ends one), a vault's while vaulting, a dive's while diving and for the second of its slide. A bank of distance fills at 1.1 times that, holds a second of it (of walking at least) plus 4 studs for late position updates, and once overdrawn puts the character back where it last moved legally (a warning at most every 5 s, with where). A new character is checked from its spawn point; when bullet time slows a character, the old scale counts for a second. It doesn't look at height or walls (a blink of up to the bank is possible). Server code that moves a character itself calls `StanceService.moved(character)` first (the check trusts it for 0.5 s), or a move over the bank is undone. Since 2026-11-20 also each character's sprint stamina (`VitalsRules`, by its class now): a sprint that runs dry is ended (Forced, "Tired") and none starts before a quarter (Rejected, "Tired"); the `Stamina` attribute shows it in tenths. Aiming settles after 0.15 s times the class's aim time. Since 2026-11-21, while a skill stops sprinting (Bulletproof) none starts (Rejected, "NoSprint") and one under way ends (Forced, "NoSprint"). |
| `LoadoutService` | Builds guns as models, hangs them on the body (a carried two-handed gun by its middle, `carryOffset`), hold modes, dual wield, drop, pickup, switching (the two-handed gun handed over partway through, `handOver`), reloads. Since 2026-11-20 it arms a character with the guns it is given (the selected loadout; the first one-handed gun in hand) and keeps `LoadoutWeight` and `WeightClass` on it in step with the guns it carries (`refreshWeight`: picked up, dropped, or lost to arms or dual wielding). Since 2026-11-21 the items' weight counts too, each gun spawns with the spare magazines the items give (two with the Ammo Pouch), and reloads and draws take Quick Draw's share of their time. Since 2026-11-22 a spawned gun wears its owner's skin for it (`makeGun(weaponId, skinId)`, `setup`'s `skins`; `ShopConfig.dress`), which it keeps when dropped. |
| `WeaponServer` | Firing: fire rate, ammo, the gun's path and obstruction, spread with modifiers, hits, damage, adrenaline. Also takes hold, drop, aim and reload requests. Since 2026-11-21 each pellet's damage is scaled by `StatusEffects.damageScale` (by the hit part and the pellet's direction), no ammo is used under Open Tab or in a Last Stand with the Ammo Pouch, and adrenaline counts the health the hits really took (what `LimbService.damage` returns: nothing from a body in a Last Stand or a limb at nothing, never more than a limb had). |
| `LimbService` | Limb damage, painkillers, arm effects. Sends killing blows to `LastStandService`. Since 2026-11-20 also regeneration (`regenerate`, every 0.25 s): after the class's quiet spell, limbs out of the red heal to full; never in a Last Stand or dead. Since 2026-11-21 items' regeneration modifiers, the painkillers a life starts with (`startingPainkillers`: the Pill Case's two more), and for Second Wind `heal`, `isHurt` and `givePainkillers` (never past the life's start). `damage` returns the health it really took (0 when ignored), which the shooter's adrenaline counts. |
| `LastStandService` | Decides Last Stand or death, runs the window, success and failure. Announces every death it deals with whoever dealt the blow (`Killed`: character, killer), which the match's scores read. Since 2026-11-21 a stand needs no adrenaline with the Pacemaker, and forfeits the meter from its start (`AdrenalineService.forfeit`). Since 2026-11-22 it counts only the segments a running skill hasn't claimed (`AdrenalineService.free`). |
| `GameServer` | Starts `ProfileService`, `MatchService`, `ProgressionService`, `ArmoryService`, `SkillService` (since 2026-11-21), `ShopService` (since 2026-11-22) and `SpawnService`, in that order. |
| `ProfileService` | Every player's saved profile (level, XP, cash, prestige, titles, stats, and since 2026-11-20 the saved loadouts and the guns bought), and **the only code that saves data**. Loads and locks the profile when the player joins, saves it every 60 s, on leaving and on shutdown; kicks a player whose profile can't be loaded instead of giving them a fresh one. A last save that fails is tried again every 10 s for as long as the server runs, and a rejoin to the same server waits for it (up to 90 s); one player's loads run one at a time (2026-11-19). `get(player)` returns it frozen; `update(player, change)` is the only way to change it (the change is kept whole or not at all, and refused if it breaks a rule); `release(player)` before a teleport; `beforeRelease(callback)`, run just before the last save, while `update` still works (play time goes in there); `Loaded` and `Changed` signals. See [[Menus, Loadout, Progression and Shop - To Do#9. Server-side economy and anti-exploit]]. |
| `ProfileSchema` | The profile's fields and defaults, its `VERSION` and migrations, `validate` (types, ranges, storable as JSON). Checks itself. Since 2026-11-20 also `Loadouts` (five, each a name of 1 to 20 characters and a gun id or nothing per slot), `SelectedLoadout` (1 to 5, or a preset's id) and `Unlocks.Guns` (bought gun ids). Gun and preset ids (up to `LoadoutRules.ID_LENGTH`) aren't checked against the game here, so one taken out of the game can't stop a profile loading. `reconcile` mends the saved loadouts as a profile loads (a missing or broken one gets its default, a name is tidied or put back to its default, a bad slot is emptied, a bad selection goes back to the first, loadouts past `SAVED` are kept), so nothing in them can refuse a profile and `SAVED` or the name length can change without a `VERSION`. Since 2026-11-21 `Unlocks` holds `Items` and `Skills` (bought ids) besides `Guns`, and each saved loadout optional `Head`, `Chest`, `Gear` and `Skill` ids (checked and mended like the gun slots); older saves load with none bought. Since 2026-11-22 `Skins` (`Owned`: skin ids; `Equipped`: a skin id per gun id) and `Crates` (crates held: a whole number from 1 to 999 per crate id), at most 1,000 skins; ids aren't checked against the catalogue, so a skin taken out of the shop can't stop a profile loading, and older saves load with none. |
| `ProfileStorage` | The DataStore side: one key per player, session locking inside `UpdateAsync` (a lock is free after 180 s without a save; any other session's lock blocks a load, even an earlier one from the same server, since 2026-11-19), retries with backoff. Its lock rules are pure functions that check themselves; it runs against a stand-in DataStore in tests. |
| `MatchService` | Runs matches on a timer: Waiting (too few players in the fight: only `Deployed` players count, not those in the main menu, since 2026-11-19), Intermission, Running. Everyone who spawns while one runs is in it. Counts kills (from `LastStandService.Killed`, credited by `CombatLog.killerOf`), deaths, the damage each player deals other players (`CombatLog.Hurt`) and each player's time in the match; at the end `Ended` carries the results (`MatchRules.results`). Pays nothing itself: `ProgressionService` listens to `Ended`. `sitOut(player)` (2026-11-18): the player went back to the main menu, so their stretch in the match ends as if they had left the server, until they spawn again. |
| `SpawnService` | Who is in the fight and who is in the main menu (2026-11-18). `Players.CharacterAutoLoads` is off: players join into the menu and get a character when they ask (`MenuRequest` "Play"); then they are `Deployed`, respawned `RespawnTime` after each death (or after losing their character any other way) until they ask to leave (`"Leave"`: refused within 5 s of a hit, in a Last Stand or dead; `MatchService.sitOut`; PLAY then waits 10 s). Points menu players' `ReplicationFocus` at the menu camera's shot. Refusals go back on `MenuResult`. Since 2026-11-20 a dead player may hold their respawn while they change loadout on the death screen (`"Hold"`, until `"Release"` or `HOLD_LIMIT` after the death; neither is throttled: each only ever affects the sender, within that limit). Only limb health attributes count as a hit. |
| `ArmoryService` | The saved loadouts and the guns bought (2026-11-20). Answers `SelectLoadout`, `SetLoadout`, `RenameLoadout` (tidied, filtered with `TextService` for its owner, the old name kept if filtering fails; the latest rename of a loadout wins) and `BuyItem` (a gun at its level, not owned, with the Cash: taken and given in one profile update, checked again inside it), each checked against the player's own profile with `LoadoutRules` and rate-limited per kind (`RATES`: a short burst, then "TooFast", said at most once per gap; putting a default name back counts as a save, not a filtered rename); sends the owner their armory (`LoadoutEvent` "State", also on "Sync") and refusals. Each purchase is logged: a line in the server's output and a Cash sink for Roblox's economy dashboard (`AnalyticsService:LogEconomyEvent`, the game's first). `spawnLoadout(player)`: the guns a spawn gets, the selection checked again whole; the armory is sent after the spawn. Since 2026-11-21 `SetLoadout` takes a whole loadout (guns, items, skill: `LoadoutRules.checkLoadout`), `BuyItem` sells items as well as guns, `BuySkill` sells skills (both through one `buy`, sharing the "Buy" rate; the thing goes in `Unlocks.Guns`, `Items` or `Skills`), the armory sent lists everything bought, and `spawnLoadout` returns the kit (`LoadoutRules.resolveKit`). |
| `ShopService` | The shop (2026-11-22): `BuySkin` (a skin on the storefront now, not owned, with the Cash), `OpenCrate` (a crate not complete, paid with one held or its price; the skin picked inside the same profile update that pays: `ShopConfig.roll` with this server's `Random`, or the next in the crate's shown order where `PolicyService` restricts random items for the player, and until it answers; since 2026-11-23 also whether the screen showed it opening in order, refused "Changed" with the shop sent again if that no longer holds), `EquipSkin` (an owned skin that fits the gun, or nothing), each rate-limited (`RATES`: buying and opening share one) and checked against the player's own profile; purchases and openings logged (a line in the output, `AnalyticsService`'s Cash sink). Crates held for a complete crate are refunded their price (as it completes, or as the profile loads). Sends the owner their shop (`ShopEvent` "State": skins owned and equipped, crates held, whether crates open in order, the stock) on every change, at the daily turnover and on "Sync". `wearing(player)`: the skins a spawn's guns wear. Studio only: `DebugCratesInOrder` on the module. |
| `ProgressionService` | XP, Cash, levels, titles and lifetime stats, paid only from events the server saw; no remote carries an amount. A kill (`LastStandService.Killed`, the killer from `CombatLog.killerOf`) pays the killer while they are in the running match, with the bonuses the killing blow earned (headshot, shoot-dodge or bullet time, Last Stand, revenge), and an assist to everyone else who dealt a quarter of the damage; at most `PAID_PER_VICTIM` kills and assists on one victim pay per match (more still score and count in stats). A match's end (`MatchService.Ended`) pays the win or the loss to those it counted and sends each player their summary. Cash is multiplied, XP never; milestone titles unlock with the level (a player wearing their top title moves up to the new one). Play time is written every 30 s and just before the last save (`ProfileService.beforeRelease`). Tells each player what was paid (`ProgressionEvent`) and takes one request, the title shown (`SetTitle`). |
| `CombatLog` | Who hurt whom in each life: `LimbService` reports every point of limb damage here (amounts that aren't real and finite are ignored). The rewards ask what the killing blow was like and who else did damage; `killerOf(victim, killer)` names the player who dealt the killing blow, even one who has respawned since; `Hurt` announces each piece of damage, which the match counts as fighting (2026-11-19). |
| `BulletTimeService` | `TimeScale` on every character: Last Stand bubbles and player-triggered bullet time (sight checks 10 times a second, the aim penalty). Since 2026-11-21 the Bullet Time skill starts it at a level for a time (`activate(character, level, duration)`; `SkillService` pays, and ends it with the skill: `deactivate`), a Stopwatch wearer isn't slowed by it, and the meter is `AdrenalineService`'s; `characters()` lists everyone it can slow. |
| `RagdollService` | Turns any dying body into a ragdoll; runs the muscles and the killing shot's push for non-players (players' own clients run theirs). |
| `TestArena` | The gun table, target dummies and the gunman in the [[Test Arena]]. Since 2026-11-21 the gunman's hits go through `StatusEffects.damageScale` like a player's, and all of it runs in Studio only (`LIVE` at its top): a live server gets the map alone, without free guns or dummies to fill a meter on. |

### Client (StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts)
`ClientMain` starts the controllers in this order: `Hud`, `CameraController`, `CharacterController`, `CoverController`, `DodgeController`, `VaultController`, `WeaponController`, `LastStandController`, `SkillController` (since 2026-11-21), `ProceduralLayer`, `AimLayer`, `HitReactions`, `RagdollController`, `KillCamController`, `AnimationController`, then the match and progression screens (`MatchHud`, `KillFeed`, `NameTags`, `Scoreboard`, `MatchEndScreen`), and last `MenuController`, which covers the HUD and takes the input from all of them while the menu is up.

| Controller | Job |
|---|---|
| `Keybinds` | Every control in one table. The HUD's keybind list reads it. `ChangeLoadout` (2026-11-20): the death screen's gamepad button, Select (M does it on a keyboard, through `Menu`). `Skill` (2026-11-21, bullet time's keys until then): X, the middle mouse button, gamepad Select. |
| `SkillController` | The skill key and what a skill shows (2026-11-21): asks the server to fire (`SkillRequest`), saying NOT ENOUGH ADRENALINE or SKILL ALREADY RUNNING itself; on `SkillEvent` "Used" puts up the HUD's banner and, for Gut Feeling, a red marker over every other character's head, through walls, for its time; "Refused" in a short message. Keeps the HUD's skill line (the level it would fire at now) and the keybind list's name for the key. |
| `CharacterController` | The client's state machine: predicts stances, sprint and crouch keys, walk speed, jumping, the painkiller and bullet-time keys. ## Studio tools and test hooks
- `ServerStorage.Tools` (never reaches players): `AnimationImporter` builds the clips from Blender's export, and `TestHarness` records and measures play tests. Both are in the repo: `Assets/Blender/Animation/studio_import.luau` and `Tools/TestHarness.luau`. The game's own scripts are not in git, on purpose (the repo is public).
- `ServerStorage.Tools.ProfileCheats` (in the repo as `Tools/ProfileCheats.luau`): Studio-only cheats that set your own Level, XP, Cash and Prestige. Clone it into ServerScriptService for a play session, then `mp.profile({ Level = 20, Cash = 5000 })` from the harness ([[Test Arena]]). It stops at once outside Studio.
- `DebugMinPlayers`, `DebugLength` and `DebugIntermission` (numbers) on the `MatchService` module change the match's timings in Studio only: `DebugMinPlayers = 1` starts a match alone, once the player has pressed PLAY (players in the menu don't count). Set them in play mode, so they aren't saved with the place. `DebugNpcKills = true` there makes the arena's dummies score and pay like players (and damage to them counts as fighting), and `DebugMinContestants = 1` lets a match played alone be won (2026-11-19).
- Characters only spawn when asked for (2026-11-18): in a play test press Enter on PLAY, or `mp.play()` from the harness; `mp.leave()` goes back to the menu.
- A Vector2 `DebugLook` attribute on the player (yaw and pitch in radians) points their camera, in Studio only. Automated play tests use it to aim. It only applies when the value changes.
- `DebugBulletTime = true` on any character activates its bullet time (it needs `Adrenaline`; a dummy needs an `Aim` toward its target). Since 2026-11-21 `SkillService` answers it: Bullet Time at the full segments, owned or not, spending them.
- `DebugCratesInOrder = true` on the `ShopService` module (Studio only, set in play mode) makes every crate open in its shown order, as where Roblox restricts random items (2026-11-22).
- `StanceService._records` exposes the server's stance records to game scripts in Studio. The command bar's `require` makes its own copy of a module, so it can't see them. Since 2026-11-21 so do `StatusEffects._records` (each character's sources) and `SkillService._records`.
