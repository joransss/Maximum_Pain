# Dual-Wield Sidearms

## Design Goal
Offer a close-range sidearm playstyle with a real trade-off, not a strict upgrade over a two-handed weapon.

## Proposed Trade-Offs
- High close-range pressure and a distinctive firing rhythm
- Worse accuracy and recoil control at medium/long range
- Slower reload or a limited ammunition pool
- Uses the secondary slot and cannot be paired with a rifle while equipped

## Tasks
- [ ] Prototype with two sidearms using a shared weapon definition
- [ ] Decide alternating shots vs simultaneous shots
- [ ] Validate each shot, ammo count and fire cadence on the server
- [ ] Ensure animation timing matches the actual shot events
- [ ] Test against the standard pistol and automatic rifle at multiple ranges

## Balance Rule
Dual-wielding must have a clear weakness. Do not balance it only by making the animations look slower; enforce accuracy, reload and slot trade-offs mechanically.
