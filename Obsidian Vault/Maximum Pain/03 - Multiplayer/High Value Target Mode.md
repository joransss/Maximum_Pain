# High-Value Target Mode

## Concept
One or two players become high-value targets. Other players score for hunting them; targets score for surviving and eliminating pursuers.

## Design Considerations
- Target role should rotate or be assigned by a fair rule.
- Targets can receive a distinctive ability or limited recovery resource, but should not become unkillable.
- Give pursuers clear target location information with intentional limits (for example, periodic pings rather than permanent wall tracking).
- Keep the mode separate from the first prototype until core combat and team modes are stable.
