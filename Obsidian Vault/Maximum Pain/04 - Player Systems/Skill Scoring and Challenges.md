# Skill Scoring and Challenges

## Match Score Events
Possible bonus events include:
- Headshot elimination
- Consecutive eliminations without dying
- Elimination while shoot-dodging
- Elimination during Bullet Time
- Objective capture, defence or assist
- Comeback or clutch round win

## Design Rules
- Keep base elimination and objective rewards meaningful; do not make flashy actions mandatory to compete.
- Score events must be awarded by server-validated combat/objective events.
- Prevent duplicate scoring from repeated damage or duplicate remote requests.
- Use short, readable on-screen callouts without obscuring the fight.

## Challenge Examples
- Win three rounds
- Capture two objectives
- Get five sidearm eliminations
- Use Shoot-dodge to avoid damage and survive
