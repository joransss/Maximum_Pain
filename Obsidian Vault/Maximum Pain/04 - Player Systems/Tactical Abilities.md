# Tactical Abilities

## Goal
Let players choose one secondary ability that changes their approach without creating pay-to-win loadouts.

## Candidate Abilities
- **Adrenaline Surge:** temporarily increases Bullet Time meter gain; does not directly increase weapon damage.
- **Team Focus:** briefly reduces the duration or strength of enemy slow effects for nearby teammates.
- **Disruption:** briefly obscures selected enemy HUD information, without disabling basic controls or hiding critical fairness cues.
- **Heavy Trigger:** improves handling briefly but applies a clear recoil/recovery drawback afterward.

These are original design concepts. Tune them around counterplay and readability.

## Rules
- Equip one ability at a time.
- Abilities use server-validated cooldowns/resources and have visible telegraphs.
- Do not sell damage boosts or exclusive combat advantages for Robux.
- Ability data should be configuration-driven, not duplicated across scripts.
