# Max Payne 3 Game Modes — Roblox Engagement

Every Max Payne 3 multiplayer mode, what it does, and which ones would keep the most players on Roblox. Researched 2026-11-10.
**Confidence:** ✅ official source · 📰 previews, reviews or guides (mostly 2012) · ❓ sources disagree or unconfirmed.
Related: [[Max Payne 3 Mechanics Reference]] · [[Gun Skin Ideas]] · [[Reference Games]]

> Maximum Pain stays original: the modes below are **inspired by** Max Payne 3. Names, characters and rules get our own versions (see the last column of the shortlist).

## Verdict
**Launch with:** Team Deathmatch + Free-for-All, then **Hunted** (our take on Payne Killer). Add **Showdown** (short round-based elimination, from the Gang Wars "Last Man Standing" round and the RIVALS duel format) soon after launch.
**Rotate as events:** **Live Wire** (our Run and Stun: infection) and **Heat Streak** (our Time Attack).
**Later:** **Turf Feud** (Gang Wars cut to 3 rounds), **Last Stand Co-op** (Dead Men Walking: wave survival).
**Fold into other modes, not standalone:** Marked Man → a vendetta bounty inside FFA/TDM. Hardcore → a playlist toggle once there are enough players.

Why: Roblox's own analytics docs say **D1 retention and average session time** matter most, and players must have fun **within the first five minutes**. Modes that drop a player straight into fighting, work with any lobby size and finish in minutes win on Roblox. Long, multi-round team modes lose players who leave mid-match.

## Every Max Payne 3 mode

### Base game (May 2012)
| Mode | Rules | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Deathmatch** | Free-for-all; eliminate opponents. | 📰 ludo.guide |
| **Team Deathmatch** | Two teams, up to 16 players. | 📰 GamesRadar, everyeye.it |
| **Hardcore** variants | Free aim only, no health regen, friendly fire on, no minimap; Last Man Standing (self-revive) off; Bullet Time much weaker. | 📰 ludo.guide, Mechanics Reference |
| **Payne Killer** | Starts as a free-for-all standoff. The player who kills Max **becomes** Max (a second player becomes Passos, his partner). Max and Passos get much more health and Bullet Time and spawn with strong guns; everyone else respawns fast and hunts them. Hunters score per hit on Max/Passos (preview: 1 point per hit up to 10, 6 for a melee hit). The next Max is picked by who landed the kill and dealt most damage. A "variation of tag". | 📰 GamesRadar hands-on, ludo.guide; ❓ exact points from a preview |
| **Gang Wars** | Two gangs, **five rounds**. Each round's objective depends on who won the last one (e.g. defend a turf → next round the losers try to plant a bomb at your base). Objective types: turf grab (capture and hold), last man standing (eliminate all), bomb disposal (plant/defuse), grab the bag (capture-the-flag), assassination (kill a marked player). Final round is a deathmatch where points from earlier rounds count. Max narrates each round over comic-style panels. A trailer mentions a pool of ~10 round types. Cash wagers before rounds. | 📰 ludo.guide, bit-tech review, MP1st trailer recap, PC Gamer; ❓ full list of the 10 types not found |

Unlocking: one reviewer had to get 50 Deathmatch kills before Payne Killer and Gang Wars unlocked. 📰 (GamesRadar)

## What works on Roblox right now
- **RIVALS** is the biggest Roblox shooter (≈200K–310K concurrent in early 2026, peak ≈964K). It leads with **round-based duels** (1v1 to 5v5, first to 5 rounds) and a ranked ladder. It was in Roblox's top 10 in 11 of 12 months of 2025. Its pace is "slightly slower than typical Roblox shooters", focused on aim and positioning. (RoWatcher, Newzoo via GameDev Reports)
- **Hypershot**, the #2 shooter (≈17–20K concurrent, peak 87K), leads with Team Deathmatch, CTF, Free-for-All, Gun Game and duels. (RoWatcher)
- Other top shooters by player count are duel or arena games (Deagle Arena, One Tap, Sniper Arena, Knife Duels). Arsenal (Gun Game) was the top FPS by visits until RIVALS passed it in May 2025. (RoWatcher)
- Outside shooters, **asymmetric "one against everyone" rounds** are a proven Roblox format: Murder Mystery 2 (≈200K–545K concurrent, 1.1M event peak) and Flee the Facility. (RoWatcher, StudioKrew)
- Roblox guidance: fun within 5 minutes; for D7 add progression and **new modes**; for D30, small updates every 2–4 weeks and big ones every 2–3 months, plus PvP, tournaments and leaderboards. (Roblox Creator Hub)

❓ No public source gives per-mode retention numbers for any Roblox shooter. The ranking below is a judgement from the evidence above.

## Shortlist and scores
Scored 1–5 (5 is best). **Fun in 5 min:** time from joining to fighting. **Any lobby:** works with 2 players or 16, and with players joining mid-match. **Short:** match length. **Identity:** shows off shoot-dodge, bullet time and dual wielding. **Comeback:** a reason to play again (roles, ranks, rivalries). **Build cost:** 5 = cheap.

| Rank | MP3 mode | Fun in 5 min | Any lobby | Short | Identity | Comeback | Build cost | Total | Our version |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payne Killer | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **28** | **Hunted**: kill the Marked pair to become them |
| 2 | Deathmatch / TDM | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | **27** | Free-for-All, Team Deathmatch (the baseline every shooter needs) |
| 3 | Gang Wars "last man standing" round (+ RIVALS duels) | 4 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | **26** | **Showdown**: 1v1–3v3, no respawns, first to 5 rounds, ranked later |

Notes on the top picks:
- **Hunted (Payne Killer)** is the best fit for this game. Everyone always has a goal (become the hunted), the hunted pair are showcases for bullet time and dual wielding, and it matches the "one against everyone" format Roblox players already know. Original version: no Max or Passos; the first two kills of the match make "the Marked" pair, who get a visible glow, bonus health and a full bullet time meter, and whoever kills one of them takes their place.
- **Showdown** turns a Gang Wars round into the format RIVALS proved: short rounds, no respawn, a ranked ladder later. Shoot-dodge and Last Stand give each round big moments.
- **Live Wire** works as a limited-time event (RIVALS ran a Halloween zombie mode the same way). Our version: one "Live Wire" player with a stun weapon; anyone stunned and finished joins them.
- **Full Gang Wars** is Max Payne 3's most praised mode, but five rounds take too long, need full teams and are hard to teach. Cut it to three rounds and add it once player counts allow.

## Engagement hooks to add to every mode
- **Vendettas** (from Max Payne 3): after the same player kills you 3 times, mark them for a bounty. Cheap, makes rivalries.
- **Wagers** (from Gang Wars): bet in-game cash on a round. Use in-game currency only — never Robux (Roblox paid random items rules).
- **Streak scoring** (from Time Attack) in every mode's scoreboard.
- **Short matches:** aim for 5–8 minutes, with join-in-progress.
