# Reference Games

Use these games as design case studies. Record observations from gameplay footage or personal play; do not treat an impression as verified implementation detail. Make an original version that fits Maximum Pain's scope and Roblox constraints.

## Max Payne 3 — primary identity reference
- Cinematic third-person gunplay
- Shoot-dodge movement and commitment
- Bullet-time presentation
- Strong combat feedback, weapon handling and pacing
- Official screenshots: https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/25o241181oo49k/new-max-payne-3-screenshots.html
- Dual-wielding screenshots: https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/25o241181ok413/new-max-payne-3-screenshots-the-art-of-dual-wielding.html
- Store page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/204100/Max_Payne_3/

## Counter-Strike 2 — competitive clarity
- Weapon recoil and accuracy expectations
- Map sightlines, cover and chokepoints
- Readable combat feedback and competitive consistency
- Store page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/730/CounterStrike_2/

## THE FINALS — objectives and destruction
- Environmental destruction as a tactical choice
- Objective readability during chaotic team fights
- Destruction feedback and changing routes
- Store page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2073850/THE_FINALS/

## Call of Duty — multiplayer presentation
- Kill feeds, killcams and hit feedback
- Weapon switching, reload pacing and respawn flow
- Match HUD hierarchy and player feedback
- Research a specific release rather than assuming every title has the same systems.

## Roblox shooter references

These are comparative references, not templates to copy. Test the actual current experience and capture your own notes; game updates may change mechanics or availability.

### Phantom Forces — weapon depth and combat readability
- [Play on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/292439477/Phantom-Forces)
- Study: weapon variety, recoil and ballistics, attachment trade-offs, movement, hit feedback, map sightlines and cover.
- **Apply to Maximum Pain:** use as a benchmark for distinct weapon behaviour and meaningful loadout choices, while keeping the camera and core loop third-person.
- ![Phantom Forces gameplay reference](https://cdn.cheat.gg/images/5fdd5066741c7.webp)
- Image source: [Phantom Forces gameplay screenshot](https://cdn.cheat.gg/images/5fdd5066741c7.webp) (third-party hosted screenshot; rights remain with its owner).

### FRONTLINES — atmosphere and weapon presentation
- [Play on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/5938036553/FRONTLINES)
- Study: weapon animation transitions, layered reloads, sound, material response, lighting and depth in industrial spaces.
- **Apply to Maximum Pain:** prioritize readable reload timing, distinct weapon audio and strong light/shadow composition without sacrificing enemy visibility.
- ![FRONTLINES warehouse gameplay reference](https://tr.rbxcdn.com/180DAY-8dbce6d7eaeb915c5eb707d2cbfec07f/768/432/Image/Webp/noFilter)
- Image source: [FRONTLINES on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/5938036553/FRONTLINES).

### RIVALS — tight competitive pacing
- [Play on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/17625359962/RIVALS)
- Study: short rounds, duel readability, compact arenas, fast weapon response, clear hit/kill feedback and progression rewards.
- **Apply to Maximum Pain:** keep match goals and combat feedback legible even when bullet time, shoot-dodge and third-person movement add visual complexity.
- ![RIVALS gameplay reference](https://media.dotesports.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Roblox-Rivals-2v2-1.jpg?w=640)
- Image source: [Dot Esports — RIVALS screenshot](https://dotesports.com/roblox/news/roblox-the-hunt-rivals-mega-token-guide).

### Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5 — tactical spaces and team coordination
- [Play on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/2916899287/Blackhawk-Rescue-Mission-5)
- Study: large environments, team movement, military equipment silhouettes, attachment customization and combat audio.
- **Apply to Maximum Pain:** use its environmental scale and team positioning as contrast to smaller urban combat arenas; do not let map scale dilute encounter pacing.
- ![Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5 gameplay reference](https://tr.rbxcdn.com/180DAY-1abf71eaba86609d3e3289bce0933c02/768/432/Image/Webp/noFilter)
- Image source: [Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5 on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/2916899287/Blackhawk-Rescue-Mission-5).

### Criminality — gritty close-range combat
- [Play on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/4588604953/Criminality)
- Study: hostile urban mood, close-range encounters, melee/firearm mix and risk-heavy combat pacing.
- **Apply to Maximum Pain:** borrow principles of tension and environmental storytelling, not its exact tone, systems or assets.
- Current experience details can change; check the live page before treating it as an availability benchmark.

### Bad Business — movement and loadout experimentation
- [Roblox search for Bad Business](https://www.roblox.com/discover/?Keyword=Bad%20Business)
- Study: weapon customization, movement options, loadout identity and progression feedback.
- **Apply to Maximum Pain:** ensure each weapon class and mobility option creates a readable tactical trade-off rather than a flat power increase.

## Comparison priority for Maximum Pain
1. **Phantom Forces:** weapon feel, recoil, attachments and hit registration.
2. **FRONTLINES:** weapon presentation, audio and lighting.
3. **RIVALS:** match pacing and readable competitive combat.
4. **Criminality:** gritty urban atmosphere and close-range tension.
5. **Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5:** team movement, environmental scale and equipment silhouettes.
6. **Bad Business:** mobility and loadout progression.

For the project's defining mechanics, continue using **Max Payne 3** as the primary reference for third-person gunplay, bullet time and shoot-dodge. Roblox FPS references are supporting case studies, not substitutes for the game's third-person identity.
