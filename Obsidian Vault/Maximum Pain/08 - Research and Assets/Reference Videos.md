# Reference Videos

A curated watchlist for analysing Roblox shooter mechanics relevant to **Maximum Pain**. These are external video links; Obsidian needs an internet connection to open or embed them. Use the links as observation material, not as permission to reuse footage, audio, animations or assets.

## Selected gameplay videos

### FRONTLINES — reloads, sound and industrial lighting
- [Why This Roblox FPS Feels So Real — FRONTLINES](https://quietspawn.com/watch/frontlines/)
- Focus: reload animation continuity, weapon settling, audio layers, spatial depth and player visibility in dark scenes.
- Note: the publisher states that the footage is archived gameplay, not a test of the current game build.

### RIVALS — duel pacing and feedback
- [RIVALS gameplay video (GamerBlox, June 2026)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AgCgMPMwRRM)
- [More RIVALS gameplay on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Roblox+RIVALS+full+gameplay)
- Focus: time-to-kill, duel transitions, target tracking, hit feedback and how compact spaces support repeated engagements.

### Phantom Forces — weapon handling and map flow
- [Phantom Forces gameplay search on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Phantom+Forces+Roblox+full+gameplay+weapon+recoil)
- [Phantom Forces official Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/292439477/Phantom-Forces)
- Focus: recoil patterns, movement, weapon switching, cover, long sightlines and the relationship between weapon class and map scale.

### Criminality — urban tension and close-range combat
- [I Spent 7 Days In Roblox Criminality — tvdisko](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cH579UuDydU)
- [More Criminality gameplay on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Roblox+Criminality+combat+gameplay)
- Focus: threat anticipation, close-range encounter rhythm, environmental mood and how players navigate contested spaces.

### Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5 — team movement and tactical environments
- [Blackhawk Rescue Mission 5 — GameLoaded Entertainment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OnJ30txj7s)
- [More BRM5 gameplay on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Blackhawk+Rescue+Mission+5+gameplay+team+combat)
- Focus: team spacing, equipment silhouettes, approach routes, long-range visibility and sound cues.

### Bad Business — movement and loadout identity
- [Bad Business gameplay search on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Roblox+Bad+Business+full+gameplay+movement+loadouts)
- Focus: movement options, weapon identity, loadout trade-offs and progression presentation. Confirm current gameplay because older videos may show outdated systems.

## How to take notes
For each clip, record:
1. Timestamp and feature observed.
2. What the player can read from animation, sound, camera and UI.
3. What creates skill expression or a meaningful trade-off.
4. One original Maximum Pain implementation idea.
5. A testable criterion (for example, target remains visible during a shoot-dodge).
