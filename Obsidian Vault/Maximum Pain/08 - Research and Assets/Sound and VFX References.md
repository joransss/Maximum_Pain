# Sound and VFX References

## Desired Feel
- Clear, restrained muzzle flash
- Distinct hit feedback
- Readable reload cues
- Short ability activation cue
- Bullet-time-inspired audio filtering / tonal shift
- Impact effects that do not obscure the target

## Asset Checklist
- [ ] Source is original or properly licensed
- [ ] Audio is balanced against gameplay
- [ ] Effects do not hide opponents
- [ ] Effects are cleaned up
- [ ] Performance tested with multiple players
