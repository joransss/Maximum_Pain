# Max Payne 3 Mechanics Reference

Everything found about how Max Payne 3 plays, single player (SP) and multiplayer (MP). Compiled 2026-10-30.
**Confidence:** ✅ official source or seen in official footage · 📰 previews, reviews or guides from 2012 · ❓ conflicting or unconfirmed.
Related: [[Max Payne 3 Gunplay - Accuracy and Recoil]] · [[Max Payne 3 Animation Reference]] · [[Max Payne 3 HUD and Combat UI Reference]]

## Movement
- **Third-person over the shoulder.** When you aim, the camera pulls in close to the shoulder. 📰 (Game Informer on the Targeting video)
- **Physics-driven movement:** Rockstar's RAGE engine with NaturalMotion's Euphoria. The body reacts to its surroundings. ✅ (Wikipedia, MCV interview)
- **Weight and momentum:** in MP, heavier loadouts mean slower movement, less stamina, and slower health regen. 📰 (Game Informer, Digital Trends)
- **Stamina (MP):** a meter limits how long you can run and roll. 📰 (TheSixthAxis)

## Shoot-dodge
- **Dive in any direction** while shooting. In SP the world slows until you touch the ground, and dives are unlimited (they don't use the bullet time meter). ✅📰 (Engadget, GamesRadar)
- **Physics and poses mixed:** "physics and an animation pose library running together"; the body behaves "realistically depending on his environment". ✅ (Rob Nelson, art director, MCV)
- **Collisions:** the body collides with walls and slides along floors. Hitting a wall ends the dive. 📰 (WhatCulture, Reviewed)
- **On the ground:** you stay down until you press a direction, and can "turn and roll as you aim in a full 360-degree arc". You're an open target while down. 📰 (Reviewed, Engadget)
- **Getting up** is a plain stand-up that leaves you exposed. 📰 (Reviewed)
- **Diving over cover:** Max lands in a position that uses the cover. 📰 (PC Invasion)
- ❓ **In MP**, whether a dive runs in real time or slow motion isn't stated anywhere I found.

## Bullet time
- **SP:** a meter you spend to slow time and pick shots; kills refill it. ✅📰
- **MP: a burst that spreads by line of sight.** Anyone in the user's line of sight is slowed, and so is anyone in *their* line of sight (a chain). Breaking line of sight frees you. The user is slowed less than their victims and stays in it until it runs out. Tier 3 extends it to the user's whole team. 📰 (Game Informer, TheSixthAxis, Digital Trends)
- ❓ Whether teammates get caught in the chain varies by source.

## Cover
- **Snap to cover** behind objects; pop out to aim. The reticle only shows while actually aiming, so you can't line up shots from full cover without exposing yourself. ✅📰 (Wikipedia, Reviewed)
- **Blind fire** and **vaulting** over cover. 📰 (GameSpot interview: "the ability to vault over cover")
- Seen in footage: kneeling behind a desk, popping up to fire, ducking with the head well below the edge; leaning out from a pillar. ✅ (Design & Tech Video 2, 2:55–3:02)

## Health
- **SP:** no regeneration; you take painkillers manually. ✅ (Wikipedia)
- **MP:** slow regeneration, which heavy loadouts reduce or remove. Painkillers in Payne Killer mode. 📰 (Digital Trends, Game Informer)
- **Last Stand (SP):** when health runs out and you have a painkiller, you get a slow-motion grace period to kill whoever shot you. Success means you survive, but it uses up all your bullet time. ✅ (Wikipedia)

## Weapons
- **Carry three:** two one-handed and one two-handed. The two-handed weapon goes in the left hand or under the arm while you fire a pistol. Dual-wielding the one-handers means dropping the two-handed weapon. ✅ (Wikipedia, GamesRadar guide)
- **MP weapon ranks** go up to 10; one-handed weapons can be dual-wielded at rank 9 (another source says rank 6). Attachments by slot: barrel, rail, underslung, internal. 📰❓ (mp3multiplayer blog; GamesRadar guide)
- **Classes:** pistols, assault rifles, sniper rifles, one- and two-handed SMGs, one- and two-handed shotguns, explosives (RPG, grenade launcher), thrown items (frag, flash, smoke, tear gas). 📰 (GameRevolution)
- Accuracy and recoil: see [[Max Payne 3 Gunplay - Accuracy and Recoil]].

## Aiming
- **Three modes:** Hard Lock (strongly pulled to targets), Soft Lock (lightly pulled), Free Aim (no help). **MP lobbies are split by aim mode** (Soft Lock vs Free Aim). ✅📰 (NVIDIA tweak guide, Kotaku, Digital Trends)
- **Reticle:** a white dot that turns red over a target; an "x" marks a kill. Optionally, a weapon-specific crosshair or circle sized to the weapon's spread or blast radius. ✅ (NVIDIA tweak guide)
- On PC, the cursor slows near targets ("Target Slowdown") and tracks moving targets ("Target Tracking"). ✅ (NVIDIA)

## Melee
- Close-range melee attacks exist in SP and MP; a kick to the head is mentioned in MP. 📰 (Wikipedia, TheSixthAxis)
- DLC Gorilla Mask: "extra adrenaline for melee kills from behind". ✅ (Wikipedia)

## Multiplayer systems
- **Up to 16 players.** Maps and modes "dynamically change within a match". ✅ (Wikipedia)
- **Modes:**
  - Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, and a Hardcore variant: free aim only, no regen, friendly fire on, no minimap. 📰
  - **Payne Killer:** two players are Max and Passos and everyone else hunts them. 📰
  - **Gang Wars:** five rounds of objectives that change based on who won the last round. 📰
  - DLC modes: Dead Men Walking, Run and Stun, Marked Man, Time Attack. ✅
- **Adrenaline and bursts:** adrenaline builds through kills, other actions and looting, and fills three tiers. Full tiers survive death; partial progress is lost. Each burst has three levels. ✅📰
- **Burst list:**

| Burst | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bullet Time | Slows everyone in your line of sight, chaining through theirs. |
| Big Dog | Boosts health for you and your team. |
| Intuition | Shows enemy positions. |
| Trigger Happy | Stronger weapons and ammo for a while. |
| Sneaky | You appear as a friendly to enemies for 10 s. |
| Paranoia | Enemies see their teammates as foes; at tier 3, friendly fire is switched off. |
| Weapon Dealer | Your team has unlimited ammo for 10 s. |
| Weapon Double Dealer | Removes enemies' weapon attachments. |
| Deadly Force, Unstoppable (DLC) | Unstoppable adds to defence. |
| Hangover (DLC) | Enemies spawn with less stamina and health, and blurred vision. |
| Explosive booby trap (DLC) | A booby trap. |
| "Slippery character" (DLC) | Lets you escape a deadly firefight. |

- **Loadouts:** up to five custom loadouts.
  - Head: a helmet takes 50% less head damage.
  - Body: medium armour blocks 25% of damage at the front and 10% at the back; heavy armour blocks 33% and 25%.
  - Gear: for example, an ammo pouch (+50% ammo) and a quick holster (faster reloads).
  - Two thrown items per spawn.
  - A weight bar trades protection for speed, stamina and regen.

  📰 (mp3multiplayer blog, Digital Trends)
- **Looting:** crouch over a body for about a second to take ammo, cash, painkillers, adrenaline and XP. In DLC, corpses can be booby-trapped. 📰✅
- **Vendettas:** after the same player kills you 3+ times, you can mark them for a bounty; whoever wins the next fight takes it. 📰 (TheSixthAxis)
- **Crews:** groups of up to five through Rockstar Social Club, with crew XP and leaderboards. ✅
- **Balance patches:**
  - Rockstar, 2012: DE .50 "accuracy and effective range slightly increased"; .38 Revolver range increased; RPG ammo cut and no more ricochets; suppressors cut damage at range. ✅ (Rockstar Newswire)
  - Fan transcription of the same patch: Mini-30 "recoil recovery speed improved". ❓

## Presentation
- **Final kill cam** in SP: an "ultra-slow motion bullet cam" on the last kill of an encounter. Holding the trigger keeps firing into the falling body. 📰 (Reviewed)
- **Cutscenes** flow straight into gameplay with no loading screens. ✅ (Wikipedia)
- Won "Best Animation" at the 2012 Inside Gaming Awards. ✅
